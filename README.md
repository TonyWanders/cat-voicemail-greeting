## Entry

> Hi, you've reached Mittens. I'm busy knocking things off tables. Leave a message, and I'll ignore it from a sunbeam.

116 characters, within the 120-character limit. One line, no em dashes.

## Why it should win

- It is really a cat's voicemail: a greeting, a beep-era "leave a message", and a cat who will not call back.
- The joke is specific to cats: knocking things off tables, and ignoring you from a sunbeam.
- Mittens has a character of her own: busy, unbothered, in charge of the house.
- It works spoken aloud: short sentences, plain words, one clear punchline at the end.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the greeting to `/output/entry.txt` (or the path given as the first
argument, or `$OUTPUT_PATH`). Deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical
output.
